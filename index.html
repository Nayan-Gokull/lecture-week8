Your brief

The rule for this exercise.

One frame at 60fps is 16.7 milliseconds. Camera capture, VIO and image tracking spend roughly 10ms of that before your own code runs. You are budgeting what is left: 6.7ms. Every realism feature you add has a cost. You can go over — the bar on the side will turn red and show it — but you cannot lock in until you have cut back down. That is deliberate: in a real project you overspend first and optimise second, not the other way round.

You will pick from five categories: grounding, lighting, audio, legibility and rendering. Spend it the way your specific brief needs, not the way that seems cleverest in the abstract. There is no single right answer — there is a right answer for your brief.
