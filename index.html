The rule for this exercise.

Your camera capture, VIO and image tracking already spend roughly 10ms of every frame before your own code runs, whatever frame rate you are aiming for — that pipeline runs at its own pace, it does not shrink just because you asked for fewer frames a second. What is left over is yours to spend. Pick your target below and the exact number is computed for you. Every realism feature has a cost, and you can go over — the bar on the side turns red and shows it — but you cannot lock in until you cut back down. That is deliberate: in a real project you overspend first and optimise second, not the other way round.

Choose your target frame rate

This is a real decision, not a formality. A steadier target buys you headroom. A faster one buys you almost nothing to spend, because the fixed 10ms overhead eats a bigger share of a smaller frame.

Pick a target above to see your Round 1 budget.
